URBANA — Someone mentioned Springfield Wildcats in an Urbana barbershop and the room did the silence it does for weather that might become a storm.

“That’s Clark,” said the barber, which was geography and a boundary.

A Hillclimbers schedule on the wall did not blink. Conversation resumed with grain prices, a safe harbor.
