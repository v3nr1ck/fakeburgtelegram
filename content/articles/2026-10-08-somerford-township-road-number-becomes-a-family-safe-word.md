SOMERFORD — A Madison County family has started using a township road number as a safe word at reunions, a practice that confused a cousin from London until it didn’t.

“When someone says TR, we change the subject,” said a Pence.

I-70 hummed south. Nobody suggested going to Columbus. That would not have been the subject change they meant.
