WEST LIBERTY — A visitor to the Piatt Castles asked whether Mac-A-Cheek or Mac-O-Chee was “the main one,” a question locals received as if she had asked which child they loved.

“They’re both the main one,” said a volunteer, which did not help.

Ohio Caverns, which is actually in Champaign County on SR-245, was offered as a nearby option for people who like underground things. The visitor said she liked above-ground drama. She had come to the right county.
