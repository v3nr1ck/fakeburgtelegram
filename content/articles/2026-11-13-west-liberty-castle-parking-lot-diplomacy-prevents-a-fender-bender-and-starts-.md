WEST LIBERTY — Two trucks in the castle parking lot negotiated space with waves so elaborate they became an introduction.

“Mac-A-Cheek’s tighter,” said one driver.

“Mac-O-Chee’s the same if you’re bad at it,” said the other.

They went in separately and left as people who would wave on 68.
