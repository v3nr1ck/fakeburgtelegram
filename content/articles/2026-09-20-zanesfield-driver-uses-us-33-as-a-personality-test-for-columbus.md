ZANESFIELD — A Logan County man has begun judging out-of-towners by how they merge onto US-33, a test he said reveals “who they are in a grocery store.”

“If you panic, I already know,” said Ray Evilsizor.

Zanesfield does not have a grocery store large enough to complete the metaphor. Evilsizor said that was the point.
