CABLE — Asked for a New Year’s resolution, a Cable man provided a sequence involving Crybaby Bridge, a dip, and “you’ll know.”

“GPS lies out here. So do Januarys,” said Milt Ferryman.

Davey Woods was offered as a nearby confirmation landmark. A visitor asked if the bridge was really haunted in 2027. Ferryman said that was the visitor’s problem.
