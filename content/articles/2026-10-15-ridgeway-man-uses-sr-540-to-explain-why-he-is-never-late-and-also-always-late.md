RIDGEWAY — A Ridgeway driver said SR-540 makes him on time in spirit even when the clock disagrees.

“It’s a thinking road,” said Neil Johnson.

Bellefontaine appeared on the horizon. He did not speed up. The thinking, he said, was the point.
