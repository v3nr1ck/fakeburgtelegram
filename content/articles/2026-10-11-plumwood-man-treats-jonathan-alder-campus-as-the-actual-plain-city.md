PLUMWOOD — A Plumwood resident gives directions to Plain City by sending people to Jonathan Alder High School first, a method that has confused at least one Amazon driver.

“You’ll see the Pioneers. Then you’re close,” he said.

Union County’s half of Plain City was described as “the other parking lot.”
