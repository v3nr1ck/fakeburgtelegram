ENON — An Enon woman compared leftover fireworks in Clark County to whatever Christiansburg was doing, a study with a sample size of two backyards and a conclusion of war.

“Champaign’s is louder. Clark’s knows what it is,” said Beth Kizer.

Greenon Knights banners were still up in town. Kizer said knights were fine but she pays taxes in a circle, which she treated as a judging credential.
