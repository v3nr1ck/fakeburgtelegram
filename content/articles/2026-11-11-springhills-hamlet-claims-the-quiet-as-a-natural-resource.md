SPRINGHILLS — A Springhills resident listed quiet as the hamlet’s chief export, then asked a visitor not to tell Dayton.

“We don’t have a sign. That’s the sign,” he said.

A grain truck ruined the export for eleven seconds. He forgave the truck, not Dayton.
