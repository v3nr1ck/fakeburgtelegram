LONDON — A jacket held a picnic table at Madison Lake State Park for forty minutes while its owner “ran to the car,” a phrase that in practice meant a conversation in the parking lot.

“That’s held,” said a child, learning property law.

A Red Raiders blanket on the next table did not dispute jurisdiction.
