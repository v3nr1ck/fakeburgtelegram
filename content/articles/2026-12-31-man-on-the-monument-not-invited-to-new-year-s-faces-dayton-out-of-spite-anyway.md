URBANA — Organizers of a Monument Square New Year’s gathering confirmed the Man on the Monument had not been invited, a snub the statue answered by continuing to face whichever way it already faced, which locals said might as well be Dayton.

“He doesn’t need a party. He needs a scarf,” said a child, reopening Tuesday’s policy.

A Hillclimbers sticker completed a lap. The roundabout did not adjourn.
