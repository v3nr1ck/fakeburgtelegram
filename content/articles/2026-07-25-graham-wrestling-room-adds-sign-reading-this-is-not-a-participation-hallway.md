ST. PARIS — Graham High School wrestling staff posted a new laminated notice outside the wrestling room reminding visitors that state-title culture does not include emotional support excuses about dual meets.

"People kept calling close losses 'learning experiences,'" a coach said. "Learning experiences are for people who do not stack trophies."

Falcon parents generally approved, then immediately began arguing about whether the font was black enough.
