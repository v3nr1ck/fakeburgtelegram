URBANA — During the downtown tree lighting, a child looked up at the Civil War monument in the roundabout and asked whether the Man on the Monument gets a scarf.

“He looks cold,” she said, which several adults treated as a policy proposal.

A Hillclimbers band parent said the statue had survived worse winters than this one. The child asked who was in charge of worse. Nobody volunteered.
