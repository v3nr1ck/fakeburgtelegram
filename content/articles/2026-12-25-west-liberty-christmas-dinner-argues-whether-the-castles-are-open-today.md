WEST LIBERTY — Mid-ham, a guest asked if Mac-A-Cheek or Mac-O-Chee was open on Christmas, a question the table received as if she had asked which child was the extra.

“They’re both closed. They’re both the main one,” said a relative, which did not help.

Ohio Caverns, which is in Champaign County on SR-245, was offered as a nearby underground option. Someone said it was Christmas. Someone else said that was the visitor’s problem.
