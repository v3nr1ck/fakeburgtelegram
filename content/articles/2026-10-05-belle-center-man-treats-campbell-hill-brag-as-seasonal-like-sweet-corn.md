BELLE CENTER — A Belle Center man only mentions Campbell Hill between July and football season, a self-imposed ordinance he said keeps the brag “fresh.”

“You can’t lead with it in March,” said Owen White. “People aren’t ready.”

He led with Indian Lake instead, which is lower and wetter and, he admitted, more fun.
