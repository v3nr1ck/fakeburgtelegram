NORTH HAMPTON — A Clark County man spent breakfast explaining that I-70 is optional, then used it to go to a specialist in Springfield because US-40 was “in a mood.”

“I can take the National Road,” said Steve Young. “I choose not to suffer.”

He suffered a little on the ramp. He called that weather.
