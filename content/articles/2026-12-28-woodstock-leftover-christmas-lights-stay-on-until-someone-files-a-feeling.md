WOODSTOCK — Leftover Christmas lights on a Woodstock porch remained lit after the holiday, a duration the owner described as “until Epiphany,” which locals translated as until someone complains in a way that counts.

“Population is a feeling. So is December,” said Earl Bodey, standing near SR-559.

South Solon, Madison County, did not send a diplomat. Bodey took that as permission to leave them on.
