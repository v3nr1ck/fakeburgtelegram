WOODSTOCK — A Woodstock resident has calculated that the village remains larger than South Solon, Madison County, if dogs, parked tractors, and “people who used to live here in their hearts” are included.

“Population is a feeling,” said Earl Bodey, standing near SR-559.

South Solon did not send a diplomat. Bodey took that as concession.
