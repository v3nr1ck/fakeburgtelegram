LONDON — A Madison County man has begun ranking courthouse-square parking spots the way other people rank high schools, a project he described as “civic,” “necessary,” and “none of your business.”

“If you circle twice, you have already lost,” said Todd Pence, who works in a building he refused to name because naming it would “give away the system.”

London Red Raiders banners still hung from last Friday. Pence said that was irrelevant, then used them as landmarks in a nine-point parking doctrine involving US-42, a grain truck, and a moral failing he attributed to Columbus plates.

County employees walking to lunch declined to comment, which Pence logged as a win.
