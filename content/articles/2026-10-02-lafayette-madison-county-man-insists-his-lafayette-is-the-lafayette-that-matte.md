LAFAYETTE — A Madison County man acknowledged other Lafayettes exist and then spoke about them the way people speak about fake cousins.

“Ours doesn’t need a brochure,” said Tom Lewis.

He gave directions involving SR-38 and a grain elevator. The other Lafayettes, he said, could figure themselves out.
