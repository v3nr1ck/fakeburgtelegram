URBANA — An Urbana child opened a Bellefontaine Chieftains hoodie from a Logan County aunt and the living room convened without a chair.

“We have Hillclimbers,” said the dad, as if the laundry room were a stadium.

The aunt said it was warm. A Graham Falcons ornament on the tree was asked, silently, to stay out of it. The hoodie remained on the kid, which is how policy is made.
