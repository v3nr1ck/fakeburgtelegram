URBANA — A man at TeaBaggers announced a New Year’s resolution at the first coffee and retired it at the second, a duration he described as “honest.”

“I’m not going to lie to a cup,” he said.

Monument Square traffic completed a lap. A Gloria Theatre ticket stub in his pocket was from a movie he had already summarized as “fine,” which is also a resolution.
