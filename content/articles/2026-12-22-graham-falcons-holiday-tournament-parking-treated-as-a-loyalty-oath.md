ST. PARIS — Families arriving at the Graham holiday basketball tournament on US-36 were informed, without a memo, that parking choice indicated household allegiance.

“If you park like a visitor, you are a visitor,” said Dana Bodey, who has not been a visitor since 1998.

A Mechanicsburg Indians sticker in the far lot was logged as either respect or a dare. Bodey said she would know by the second quarter.
