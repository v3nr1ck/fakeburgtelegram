ENON — A Greenon Knights parent spent pregame explaining that Enon is not Springfield, a distinction that matters more the closer you get to Springfield.

“We have a mound,” she said. “They have specialists.”

The Knights’ black and white looked crisp under lights. A Southeastern Trojans fan from South Charleston nodded as if mounds were a conference.
