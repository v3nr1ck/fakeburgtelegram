WEST JEFFERSON — Locals confirmed that a honk on 1 Roughrider Drive is not the same honk as on US-40, a distinction they said out-of-towners keep getting wrong.

“One is hello. One is move. One is I went to school here,” said Dana Ward, counting on her fingers and then starting over.

The Roughriders’ brown-and-gold was visible from the National Road. A truck from London did not honk, which was read as either respect or a feud. Nobody asked the truck.
