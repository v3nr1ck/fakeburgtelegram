VALLEY HI — An Urbana man returned from Mad River Mountain and described the slope as “basically Colorado,” a comparison that did not survive a photo of Colorado.

“It’s the same sky,” said Justin Miller, whose Hillclimbers hoodie was wet in a patriotic way.

A Bellefontaine Chieftains sticker on a nearby car did not comment. Miller said Champaign County could have a ski hill if it wanted one, then admitted it did not want one.
