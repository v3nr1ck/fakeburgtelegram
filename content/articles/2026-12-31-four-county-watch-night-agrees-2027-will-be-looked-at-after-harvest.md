CHAMPAIGN COUNTY — Representatives of Champaign, Madison, Logan, and Clark, meaning four uncles at a leftover ham, agreed that 2027 would be looked at after harvest and refused to say which harvest.

“We’ll look at it,” said the Champaign uncle.

“Campbell Hill,” said Logan, which was not a date.

“I-70 makes the year look shorter,” said Clark, which Madison accepted as a concession so everyone could have pie.
