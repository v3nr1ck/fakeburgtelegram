BELLEFONTAINE — A Benjamin Logan Raiders supporter mentioned the program’s bowling state title in a hardware store on the way to SR-47, a fact he deploys like a spare key.

“People forget bowling is a sport until we win,” he said.

A Chieftains cap in the next aisle did not turn. That, too, was a kind of sport.
