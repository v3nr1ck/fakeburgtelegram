VALLEY HI — Fog settled on Mad River Mountain and a local called it “doing the work,” as if weather were on the payroll.

“Ski hill’s still a ski hill when you can’t see it,” said Amber Moore.

A Champaign County family taking the scenic way to Indian Lake pulled over to agree, then argued about whether this counted as a lake trip.
