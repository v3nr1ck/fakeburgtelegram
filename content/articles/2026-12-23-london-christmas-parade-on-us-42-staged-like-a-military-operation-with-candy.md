LONDON — The Christmas parade along US-42 assembled with the gravity of a deployment and the inventory of a candy store.

“If you throw too early, you have already lost,” said Todd Pence, holding a bag of wrapped peppermints like evidence.

London Red Raiders colors moved as a block. A float from West Jefferson Roughriders was granted a lane and watched. Pence called that diplomacy.
