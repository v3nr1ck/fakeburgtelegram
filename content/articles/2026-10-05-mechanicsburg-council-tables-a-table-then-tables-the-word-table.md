MECHANICSBURG — Village council at 18 N. Main Street tabled a discussion, then spent additional minutes on whether they should stop using the word “table” because it was doing too much.

“We’re not the city,” said a member, meaning Urbana.

A Simple Coffee cup in the room was treated as a witness. Hemisphere was not present, which someone noted.
