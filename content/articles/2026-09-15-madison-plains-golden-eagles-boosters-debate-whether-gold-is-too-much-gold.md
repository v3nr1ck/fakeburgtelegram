MIDWAY — Madison-Plains Golden Eagles boosters spent forty minutes last week on whether the word “gold” in the mascot already covered spirit-wear purchases, or whether more gold was required as a show of faith.

“It’s in the name,” said one parent.

“That’s how they get you,” said another.

The meeting was held in a building that smells like a concession stand even when it isn’t. Linson Road traffic provided the only motion. A Mechanicsburg Indians schedule on the wall was treated as foreign policy.
