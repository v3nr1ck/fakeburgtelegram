ROSEWOOD — Giving directions toward Madison County, a Rosewood farmer included London Correctional Institution as a thing you will see and should not comment on.

“It’s there,” he said. “Keep driving.”

US-29’s continuation east was described as “how you get to people who have more Amazon.” He did not mean it unkindly. He also did.
