CHAMPAIGN COUNTY — A visitor at Freshwater Farms of Ohio on US-68 North asked staff whether a particular trout might remember him from a previous feeding frenzy, a question biology answered with a hard no and emotion answered with a soft maybe.

"They remember pellets," an employee said. "Not vibes."
