LEWISTOWN — The pickup line at Indian Lake Elementary has unofficial rules now, including a no-pass clause that is enforced by looks.

“County Road 91 is not a racetrack,” said a mom, who then inched forward like a racetrack.

A Lakers bumper sticker peeled at one corner. Nobody mentioned it. That would be a different constitution.
