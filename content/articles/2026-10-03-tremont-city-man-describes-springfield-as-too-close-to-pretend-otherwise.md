TREMONT CITY — A Tremont City man said Springfield is close enough that pretending you’re “out in the county” requires commitment he no longer has.

“I can hear I-70 in my teeth,” he said.

He still tells Urbana people he lives in the country. Urbana people tell him they also live in the country. This is the regional sport.
