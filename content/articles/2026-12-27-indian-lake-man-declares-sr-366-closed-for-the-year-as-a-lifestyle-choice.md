LAKEVIEW — An Indian Lake Lakers fan announced that SR-366 was closed for the year, a claim contradicted by the road remaining open and also by his truck being on it.

“Spiritually closed,” he said.

Russells Point ice-cream traffic was mentioned as a Saturday problem from another life. He packed a chair anyway, then put it back, then packed it again.
