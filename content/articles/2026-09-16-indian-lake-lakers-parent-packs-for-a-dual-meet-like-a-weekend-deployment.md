LEWISTOWN — An Indian Lake Lakers parent loaded a truck for a midweek game as if Indian Lake itself might freeze over before overtime.

“SR-366 does not care about your timeline,” said Patty Neer, strapping down a chair that has seen things.

Russells Point was already discussing weekend boat traffic. Neer said that was a Saturday problem. Tonight was about the Lakers, the gym, and a snack policy she described as “evolving.”
