LAKEVIEW — A Lakeview woman has begun assigning moral weight to pontoon parking, a system that uses phrases like “that’s a choice” audible across two slips.

“Russells Point sees it,” she said.

An Indian Lake Lakers flag drooped in humid air. She straightened it, which she said was unrelated.
