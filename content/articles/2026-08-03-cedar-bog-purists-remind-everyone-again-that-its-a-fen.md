CHAMPAIGN COUNTY — Nature lovers on Woodburn Road near Cedar Bog Nature Preserve continued their sacred duty of reminding newcomers that the landmark is technically a fen, not a bog, a distinction that matters deeply to six people and must be explained to everyone else.

"Words mean things," said one purist, standing near rare plants and absolute certainty.
