CHAMPAIGN COUNTY — A driver on Heck Hill Road told a passenger the hill was “good for people,” a program with no brochure and no opt-out.

“Clark County has interstates. We have this,” he said.

The passenger, who had not asked for a philosophy, received one anyway.
