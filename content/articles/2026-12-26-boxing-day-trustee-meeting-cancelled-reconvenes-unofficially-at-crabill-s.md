URBANA — After the township cancelled its December 26 meeting for lack of a quorum and also for Christmas, three men met at Crabill’s anyway and called it a work session.

“Cash only. Like democracy,” said Harlan Zerkle, counting change onto the worn counter.

A Mayflowers bag at a nearby table was noted as foreign policy. Nobody took minutes. Everybody remembered.
