CHAMPAIGN COUNTY — A resident near Millerstown Road and River Road reported hearing "grinding" at midnight near the old Concord Mills area, a claim historians treat carefully and storytellers treat as content.

"It was corn," he said.

"It was wind," said his wife.

Both filed the report under marriage.
