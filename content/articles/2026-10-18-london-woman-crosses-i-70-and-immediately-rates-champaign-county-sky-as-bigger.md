LONDON — After a trip north of I-70, a London woman announced that Champaign County has a bigger sky, a finding she said was obvious from the Red Raiders parking lot.

“You can tell they don’t have an interstate,” she said.

A man from West Jefferson said sky size was a Roughrider issue too. They did not resolve it. They never do.
