PLAIN CITY — A Jonathan Alder Pioneers band parent stopped a conversation on US-42 to object to the phrase “just marching band,” a correction delivered with the calm of someone who has already packed a cooler.

“There is no just,” said Marlene Grove, holding a black-and-scarlet blanket like evidence.

The Pioneers were loading after practice. A freshman asked if this was about the music. Grove said it was about civilization.

A passing Amish buggy did not slow down. Grove respected that.
