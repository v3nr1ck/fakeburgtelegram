ST. PARIS — A woman returning a gift on Springfield Street was observed with the concern usually reserved for people who leave church during the announcement.

“It’s not the return,” said a bystander. “It’s the energy of the return.”

Ruthie’s and the Local Pair Market kept their faces professional. A Wag n Tails client with a freshly clipped dog walked past like a person with nothing to hide.
