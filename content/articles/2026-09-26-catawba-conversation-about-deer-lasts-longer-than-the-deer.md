CATAWBA — Two neighbors discussed a deer for twenty-two minutes after the deer had left, a briefing that covered SR-54, last fall, and a cousin in South Vienna.

“It looked at me,” said one.

“They all look at you,” said the other.

Both considered this a news story. The Fake Burg Telegram agrees, professionally.
