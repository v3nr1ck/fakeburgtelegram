WEST JEFFERSON — Asked where something was in town, a woman said, “You know, by the Roughriders,” which described half of West Jefferson and all of her priorities.

“Brown and gold is the landmark,” she said.

US-40 waited. I-70 waited louder. She did not take I-70. She had principles.
