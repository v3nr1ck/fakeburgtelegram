NEW CARLISLE — Tecumseh Arrows fans have started treating St. Paris–New Carlisle Road as an unofficial border, a line they say you can feel in the tires.

“Graham’s out there,” said a man who would only give his name as “not from St. Paris,” pointing north as if Falcons wrestling might appear.

Christiansburg, which sits on the Champaign side like a rumor, was mentioned three times in ten minutes. Nobody from Christiansburg was present to confirm or deny being a rumor.

A sheriff’s deputy from Clark County said the road is a road.
