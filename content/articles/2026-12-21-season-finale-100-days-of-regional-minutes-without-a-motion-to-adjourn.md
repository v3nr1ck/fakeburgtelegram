CHAMPAIGN COUNTY — After a hundred-day stretch of covering ditches, dual meets, castles, caverns, and I-70 as a personality, the Fake Burg Telegram can confirm the region still does not adjourn.

“We’ll look at it after harvest,” said a trustee, somewhere, always.

Hillclimbers, Falcons, Indians, Cardinals, Tigers, Red Raiders, Pioneers, Golden Eagles, Roughriders, Chieftains, Raiders, Lakers, Wildcats, Cougars, Jets, Warriors, Arrows, Knights, Trojans, Braves, and Irish remain unmixed. The roads remain numbered and named. The sky, according to at least one source in every county, is still bigger here.
