NEW CARLISLE — A Tecumseh Arrows household drove St. Paris–New Carlisle Road after dark for the sole purpose of scoring leftover Christmas lights like a dual meet.

“Christiansburg is doing a vibe,” said Kyle Fraley. “St. Paris is doing a program.”

Graham Falcons colors on a porch were marked as extra credit. Nobody from Champaign County had asked to be judged. Fraley said that was the point of a road.
