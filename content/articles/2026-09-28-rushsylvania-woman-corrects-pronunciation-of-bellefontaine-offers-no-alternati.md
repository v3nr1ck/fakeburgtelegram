RUSHSYLVANIA — A Rushsylvania woman corrected three different pronunciations of Bellefontaine in one afternoon and then admitted she doesn’t love any of them.

“There’s a right way. Nobody uses it,” said June Wilson.

She still corrected people. That is the job.
