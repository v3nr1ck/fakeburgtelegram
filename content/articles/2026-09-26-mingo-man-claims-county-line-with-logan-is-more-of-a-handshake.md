MINGO — A Mingo resident described the Champaign–Logan line as “more of a handshake than a border,” then got very specific about which ditch is whose.

“West Liberty’s right there,” he said, pointing at a field.

WLS Tigers pride leaked into the sentence. He said that was geography, not sports, which is a sentence only a liar or a booster would say.
