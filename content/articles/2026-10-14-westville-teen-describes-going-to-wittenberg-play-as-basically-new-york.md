WESTVILLE — A Westville teenager attended a play at Wittenberg and reported back that Springfield was “basically New York,” a claim that did not survive a map.

“They had a program,” she said.

Her parents, who have been to the Gloria Theatre, exchanged a look that has its own zip code.
