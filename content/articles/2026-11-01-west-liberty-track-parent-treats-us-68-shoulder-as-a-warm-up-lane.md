WEST LIBERTY — A WLS track parent jogged the US-68 shoulder before a meet and called it “the real warmup,” a phrase that made a sheriff’s deputy close his eyes for one second.

“Tigers don’t need a track to be Tigers,” she said, which is not how meets work.

Ohio Caverns traffic mixed in. Nobody went underground. Yet.
