SOUTH SOLON — A discussion about a stop sign drew two residents and a third opinion that arrived by text.

“We don’t need more government,” said one.

“We need the sign to mean it,” said the other.

Madison-Plains Golden Eagles youth practice let out somewhere in the distance. Both men used that as a time check.
