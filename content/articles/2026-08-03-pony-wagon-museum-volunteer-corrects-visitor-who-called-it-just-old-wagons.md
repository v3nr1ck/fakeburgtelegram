ST. PARIS — A volunteer at the Pony Wagon Town Historical Museum gently but firmly corrected a visitor who described the exhibits as "just old wagons," a phrase that lands in St. Paris the way "just football" lands on a Friday in autumn.

"It's heritage," the volunteer said, in a tone that closed the debate and opened a brochure.
