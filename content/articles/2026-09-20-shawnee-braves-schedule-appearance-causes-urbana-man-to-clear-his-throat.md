SPRINGFIELD — An Urbana Hillclimbers alumnus saw Shawnee Braves on a Central Buckeye Conference mention and made a noise usually reserved for unexpected relatives.

“Possum Road,” he said, as if the road itself had done something.

1675 East Possum Road continued to exist. He drove home on US-68 and told his wife the Braves were “still at it,” which she had not asked.
