ENON — A Champaign County woman walked the Enon Mound loop and spent the drive home on SR-4 comparing it, unfavorably and then favorably, to Melvin Miller Park in Urbana.

“I’m allowed,” said Beth Kizer. “I pay taxes in a circle.”

Greenon Knights banners were up in town. Kizer said knights were fine but Hillclimbers had more stairs in the name, which she treated as a moral advantage.
