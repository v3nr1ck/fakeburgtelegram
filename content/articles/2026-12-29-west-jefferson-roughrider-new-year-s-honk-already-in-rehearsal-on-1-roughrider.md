WEST JEFFERSON — Locals confirmed that the New Year’s honk on 1 Roughrider Drive is not the same honk as on US-40, and that rehearsal had already begun.

“One is hello. One is midnight. One is I went to school here,” said Dana Ward, counting on her fingers and then starting over.

A truck from London did not honk, which was read as either respect or a feud. Nobody asked the truck. I-70 hummed like a relative who would not leave.
