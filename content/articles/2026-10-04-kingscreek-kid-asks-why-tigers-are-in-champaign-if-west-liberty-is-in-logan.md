KINGSCREEK — A child asked why West Liberty-Salem Tigers play in Champaign County if West Liberty is in Logan County, a question that made three adults invent maps.

“The school is on 68,” said one, which is true.

“The heart is wherever the track meet is,” said another, which is also true in a way that would not survive a surveyor.
