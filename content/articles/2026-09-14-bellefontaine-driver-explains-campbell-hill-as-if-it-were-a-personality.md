BELLEFONTAINE — A Logan County resident has begun giving directions exclusively by way of Campbell Hill, Ohio’s highest point, even when the destination is a pharmacy.

“You feel it,” said Craig Huffman, who lives closer to SR-47 than to any summit.

Benjamin Logan Raiders bumper stickers outnumbered maps in his truck. Huffman said maps were for people who had not yet chosen a hill.

A visitor from Urbana asked if Champaign County had a highest point. Huffman looked pained, then offered Indian Lake as a consolation prize, which is not a hill.
