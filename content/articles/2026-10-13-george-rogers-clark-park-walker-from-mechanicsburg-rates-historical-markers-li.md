SPRINGFIELD — A Mechanicsburg woman walked George Rogers Clark Park and internally scored every marker, a system that deducted points for “too much paragraph.”

“Udney Hyde would have kept it shorter,” she said, which is not how history works and is how she works.

She ate Frosty’s in the car on the way home, which she said was not a score.
