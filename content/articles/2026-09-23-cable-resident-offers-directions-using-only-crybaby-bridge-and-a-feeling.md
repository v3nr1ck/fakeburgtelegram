CABLE — Asked how to get to a farm on Lonesome Road, a Cable man provided a sequence involving Crybaby Bridge, a dip, and “you’ll know.”

“GPS lies out here,” said Milt Ferryman.

Davey Woods was offered as a nearby confirmation landmark. The visitor asked if the bridge was really haunted. Ferryman said that was the visitor’s problem.
