SOUTH VIENNA — Northeastern Jets supporters have launched a quiet campaign to convince visitors that Bowman Road is not loud, a campaign that involves talking about it at volume.

“It’s rural,” said Kyle Fraley. “Rural is a sound.”

A grain truck passed. Fraley waited until it was gone, then continued, which he said proved his point. I-70 hummed in the distance like a relative who would not leave.
