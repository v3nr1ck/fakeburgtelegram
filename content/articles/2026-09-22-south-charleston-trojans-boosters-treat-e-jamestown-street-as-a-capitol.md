SOUTH CHARLESTON — Southeastern Trojans boosters referred to E. Jamestown Street as “downtown” in a way that made a visiting Greenon Knights parent look around for more of it.

“This is enough downtown,” said a booster.

The Trojans’ field lights came on. Someone mentioned I-70 as if it were a rumor from another life.
