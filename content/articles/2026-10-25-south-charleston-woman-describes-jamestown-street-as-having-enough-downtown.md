SOUTH CHARLESTON — A Southeastern Trojans parent said E. Jamestown Street has enough downtown, a sentence that ended a visitor’s search for more.

“Springfield has extra,” she said. “We have enough.”

The visitor, who had wanted a second coffee shop, received a philosophy instead.
