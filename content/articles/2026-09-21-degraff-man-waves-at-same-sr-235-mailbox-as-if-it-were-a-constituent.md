DEGRAFF — A DeGraff resident has been waving at a particular mailbox on SR-235 for eleven years, a relationship he described as “stable.”

“It doesn’t write back. That’s how you know it’s honest,” said Gene Ropp.

Kiser Lake traffic in season complicates the wave. Ropp said he adjusts, like a professional.
