URBANA — A Cedar Bog regular intercepted a Madison County visitor on Woodburn Road to explain, not for the first time this month, that it is a fen.

“Bog is what people say when they haven’t suffered,” he said.

The visitor said the orchids were pretty. The regular allowed that this was allowed.
