WESTVILLE — A Westville man prefers Upper Valley Pike to US-68 when going to Springfield because, he said, 68 “makes you think about your life.”

“The Pike is honest,” said Jeff Clark.

Clark County began without a sign he respected. He said he always knows anyway, because the traffic gets worse and the opinions get louder.
