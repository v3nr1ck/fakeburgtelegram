MECHANICSBURG — A Burg woman finished Christmas wrapping in Indians spirit paper last used, she said, “when the paper still meant something.”

“It’s still red. It’s still ours,” said Marlene Grove, taping a corner on High Street with the calm of a person who has already packed a cooler for a game that is not today.

A Simple Coffee cup watched from the table and declined to comment on the year.
