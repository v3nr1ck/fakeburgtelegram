URBANA — Customers waiting at TeaBaggers on N Main St spent a full 12 minutes debating whether attending Second Saturday events constitutes a lifestyle or merely proof of owning shoes that can handle brick.

"I go for the art," said one woman, who also goes for the gossip.

"I go because parking is a blood sport and I like to win," said another.

The barista continued making drinks with the neutrality of a UN observer.
