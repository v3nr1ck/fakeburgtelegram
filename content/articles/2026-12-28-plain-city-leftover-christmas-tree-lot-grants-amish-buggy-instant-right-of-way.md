PLAIN CITY — At a leftover Christmas tree lot off US-42, an Amish buggy was granted right-of-way so complete that two Pioneer band parents stopped mid-argument about whether discounted trees still counted as Christmas.

“There is no just leftover,” said Marlene Grove, holding a black-and-scarlet blanket.

The buggy did not slow down. Grove respected that. A Jonathan Alder sousaphone case in a nearby van did not comment.
